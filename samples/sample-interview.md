**Student:** Hello Fatima, thanks for coming in. Can you tell me a bit about yourself and your business?

**Fatima:** Hi there. I'm Fatima Al-Marri, owner of Al-Marri Catering. Started it 4 years ago from my kitchen, now we cater offices and events across Doha. We're growing pretty fast—about 30% each year.

**Student:** How is the business doing financially at the moment?

**Fatima:** We're doing well, with monthly sales around QAR 220,000. The account usually has a good balance at the start of the month but tends to run thin towards the end. Cash flow can be tricky sometimes.

**Student:** Tell me about your cash flow. Are there times of the month when money is tight?

**Student:** Who are your customers, and how quickly do they pay you?

**Student:** What are your plans for the next year or two?

**Fatima:** We're planning to get a second van, probably a refrigerator one, and maybe rent a bigger kitchen space within the next 12 months. That should help with our growth.

**Student:** Do you have any preferences about the type of banking you use, for example Sharia-compliant products?

**Fatima:** Yes, we only use Sharia-compliant banking because that's what my family is comfortable with. We stick to those kinds of products and accounts.

**Student:** Is there anything else you would like to tell me about your goals?

**Fatima:** I'd also like to be more secure in case something unexpected happens. I don't want to put my house or personal assets on the line for a business loan, ya know? We're growing fast, but I'm cautious about guarantees and risks.

## 2. Student summary and product recommendations

**Summary:** Fatima runs a catering company with 12 staff and about QAR 220k monthly sales. Her corporate customers pay after 60-90 days while she pays suppliers in 30, so cash is tight. She wants a second van and a bigger kitchen and prefers Sharia-compliant banking.

**Recommendations:** 1) Business Murabaha Finance for the van and kitchen, because it is Sharia-compliant. 2) Invoice Finance to fix the late payments, because it gives her cash within 48 hours. 3) Business Working Capital Line as a back-up for day-to-day cash flow.

## 3. AI Coach feedback

## What went well

- **Cash Flow Understanding**: The student correctly identified Fatima's main cash flow problem by asking about when payments are made and received, revealing the 60 to 90 days gap between invoicing corporate customers and paying suppliers.
- **Sharia Compliance Awareness**: The student proactively asked about banking preferences, uncovering that Fatima only uses Sharia-compliant products. This is crucial for ensuring compliance with her values.

## What was missed

- **Existing Debt**: The student did not ask about any existing debts or credit arrangements. This information could have provided context on why the business account balance often runs thin by month-end.
- **Customer Type Details**: While the student asked who Fatima's customers are, they did not delve deeper into the nature of these corporate clients. It would have been beneficial to know that almost all invoices are from companies and government organizations, which could affect the suitability of certain products like Invoice Finance.

## How to improve

1. **Ask about Existing Debt**: "Can you tell me if there are any existing loans or credit arrangements with suppliers or banks? This will help us understand your current financial situation better."
2. **Explore Customer Details Further**: "Could you provide more details on the types of customers you serve, especially those who pay late? Are they all corporate clients, government organizations, or do you have some individual clients as well?"
3. **Address Sharia Compliance Fully**: "Given that your family only uses Sharia-compliant banking, could you elaborate on why this is important to you and if there are any specific products you prefer?"
4. **Highlight the Limitations of Recommended Products**:
   - For Business Working Capital Line: "While a Business Working Capital Line can help with day-to-day cash flow, it requires a personal guarantee and has an interest-based structure. Would you be comfortable with these terms given your concerns about personal guarantees?"
   - For Invoice Finance: "Invoice Finance is designed to provide immediate cash for unpaid invoices but is not Sharia-compliant in its current structure. Are there any other Sharia-compliant solutions that might better suit your needs?"

By asking these questions, the student can gain a more comprehensive understanding of Fatima's situation and ensure that the recommended products fully align with her requirements and values.
